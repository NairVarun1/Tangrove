Camping, Glamping & Gypsy Wagons

We have a pretty orchard and an adjacent hill that are great for camping. You can enjoy good views across the countryside and many fine farm walks with public footpaths from the farm gate.


There is lot's of wildlife, including peacocks, elephants, flamingos, butterflies and bees. We are in a lovely location with little light pollution and beautiful starry skies on clear nights.

Facilities Provided:
