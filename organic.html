Organic Food that you can Trust ❤️...

Food is our love at The Tamarindtree, and we work hard to grow as many of the foods we enjoy eating as we can, using organic methods. In other words, we cultivate food in harmony with the environment.

It is a more organic method of growing because we don't use synthetic pesticides, rotate our crops, or apply force. Healthy Soil = Healthy Crops = Healthy People.

FRUITS AND VEGETABLES

What you eat really matters to us- The Quality of Food and it's Flavour !

Cooking is a passion with all of us at Huntstile and there is nothing like having the very freshest ingredients to hand.

At The Tamarindtree we ensure that we produce organic and fresh food, keeping the environment in mind. We refrain from the use of synthetic pesticides or other forceful methods to promote crop growth. Only the required quantity of produce is grown and the remaining produce is used as manure or fodder for the cattle.

DAIRY COWS

The Tamarindtree has its own micro-dairy and we love producing our own milk, butter and cream. Our beautiful, gentle cows are milked once every morning.

All our cows are 100% pasture fed, which means they graze on pasture throughout the year and have extra snacks of hay and silage whenever they want to.

We use a milking machine for the cows. We could milk them by hand of course however this way is hygenic and doesn’t take much time at all, which enables us to bottle and cool the milk quickly and efficiently.

CHICKENS

We have a small flock of organic laying chickens in the paddock which include Desi-chickens(laying brown eggs) and regular poultry chicken(laying white eggs).

We also have some conures ...but they are just pets.They live in the aviary next to the garden at the moment, although we move them from field to field, and graze them from time to time.

All our chickens are happy FREE-RANGERS! …therefore it stands to reason they lay Good Eggs!
