About The Tangrove

A STUNNING VENUE THAT IS PERFECT FOR ALL YOUR ACTIVITIES

Welcome to The Tangrove, where the beauty of nature meets the comfort of home. Nestled in the heart of lush countryside, our farmstay is a haven for those seeking a tranquil escape from the hustle and bustle of everyday life.

At The Tangrove, we pride ourselves on offering a unique and immersive experience that allows guests to reconnect with the simplicity of rural living. Our farm is not just a place to stay; it's a journey into the heart of nature, where the rhythm of the seasons sets the pace for a rejuvenating retreat.

Surrounded by acres of verdant landscapes, The Tangrove invites you to unwind in cozy accommodations that seamlessly blend modern amenities with rustic charm. Whether you're seeking a romantic getaway, a family adventure, or a solo retreat, our farmstay provides a serene backdrop for unforgettable memories.

Wake up to the gentle sounds of nature, breathe in the fresh country air, and embark on a day filled with exploration. Stroll through our vibrant orchards, interact with our friendly farm animals, or simply relax by the pond and enjoy the picturesque views. For those with a green thumb, we offer hands-on experiences in our organic gardens, where you can learn about sustainable farming practices and even harvest your own produce.

At The Tangrove, we believe in the power of community and the joy of shared experiences. Our communal spaces are designed to foster connections, whether around a bonfire under the starlit sky or at our farm-to-table dining area, where locally sourced ingredients come together to create delicious and wholesome meals.

Escape to The Tangrove, where the warmth of hospitality meets the tranquility of the countryside. Immerse yourself in the rhythm of rural life, and let The Tangrove be your home away from home.

Our Mission

Sustainability is the key and at Tangrove we encourage our guests and staff to join in with this too. We see it as our duty to make responsible choices and by engaging our Tangrove team and our customers to make ethical and sustainable choices we can, together, help to create a better future.

From food to insulation to lighting to waste management, we are doing what we can to reduce our impact on the world's precious resources.

FOOD

  • Respect Seasonality
  • Grow and use organic Tangrove produce as much as possible
  • Supplement with produce from other local farms

HOUSE

  • The house has been built using natural materials
  • Provide eco-friendly soaps and lotions during the stay

WATER CONSERVATION

  • Collection of rainwater for flushing toilets, making liquid comfrey fertiliser (for our delicious tomatoes) and for irrigation of vegetable gardens.
  • Water-efficient tap fittings to the highest practical level are used in the farmhouse to reduce water consumption and wastage

WASTE MANAGEMENT

  • Extensive recycling of a range of materials (glass, tins, plastics, paper & cardboard)
  • Composting ground to convert organic waste into manure.
